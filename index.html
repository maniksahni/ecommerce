NEED TO KNOW

Shopping with Shivara

How do I place an order?+

Add your favourites to the bag and send the prepared inquiry on WhatsApp. We will confirm availability, final pricing and delivery details with you.

Do you deliver across India?+

Yes, Shivara offers PAN India delivery. Exact timelines and shipping charges are confirmed with your order.

Can you help me choose a gift?+

Absolutely. Share the occasion, style and budget on WhatsApp and we will help you shortlist the right piece.

Are prices and availability live?+

Collections move quickly. The website creates your shortlist; current availability and final price are confirmed personally before the order is placed.
