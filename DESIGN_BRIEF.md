1. Concept: LIGHT ON GOLD — a quiet, cinematic jewellery showroom where each piece feels discovered under a moving pool of light.
2. Palette: near-black #0B0A09, warm ivory #F4EDE0, antique gold #C8A04A–#F0D98A, with cobalt #1E3FBF reserved for evil-eye jewellery.
3. Display type: Cormorant Garamond, oversized, tightly tracked and editorial; interface type: Manrope with measured, legible spacing.
4. Scale: hero 72–146px desktop / 74px mobile; section titles 48–104px / 52px; product titles 23–32px; utility copy 10–15px.
5. Layout: deep dark foundations, ivory editorial interruptions, deliberate asymmetry, hairline dividers and generous but disciplined whitespace.
6. Light: directional gold illumination belongs to imagery and interaction states; avoid broad decorative gradients and flat yellow fills.
7. Motion: slow image drift, line-by-line reveal, restrained parallax, pin-and-reveal storytelling, and short opacity/transform transitions.
8. Interaction: tactile hover and focus states, magnetic CTA, horizontal category browsing, tilt/glare product edits, and thumb-friendly mobile controls.
9. Performance: CSS/native animation first, lazy media below fold, one preloaded hero, no WebGL, and reduced-motion/low-power fallbacks.
10. Accessibility: keyboard-first controls, visible focus, readable contrast, descriptive alt text, no motion-dependent content, and no horizontal page overflow.
